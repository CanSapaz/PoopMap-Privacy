PoopMap — Terms of Use

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. The service

PoopMap ("the app") shows public toilets from OpenStreetMap near your location and offers navigation handoff, saved toilets, notes, an emergency card and optional offline packs. It is published by Fidovasoft. By installing or using the app you accept these terms.

2. Data accuracy

Toilet locations, opening hours, fees and accessibility details are community-maintained OpenStreetMap data and may be incomplete, outdated or wrong. PoopMap shows them as they are, with the last on-site verification date when one exists. Verify before you rely on them; the app is a finder, not a guarantee that a toilet exists, is open or is accessible.

3. The emergency card

The emergency card is a courtesy message you may show to venue staff. It is not a medical document, carries no legal right of access and does not replace schemes such as a "can't wait" card issued by patient organisations.
