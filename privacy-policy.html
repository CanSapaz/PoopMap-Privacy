PoopMap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version

PoopMap collects no data. None. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

Location

Your location is used only on your device, to show public toilets near you and to compute distances. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. Map tiles and toilet data are fetched from public servers (OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap's Overpass API); like any web request, those servers see your IP address and the map area requested, but PoopMap sends no identifiers with these requests.

Data stored on your device

Favorites, personal notes, offline area packs and settings are stored locally on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

PoopMap Pro and tips are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We operate no purchase servers and receive no personal payment information. Your Pro entitlement is tied to your Google account by Google Play and restored by Google Play.

Permissions

  • Location — only while using the app, to find toilets near you.
  • Internet — to load the map and toilet data.

That is the complete list.

Third-party services

  • Map data: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)
  • Map tiles: OpenFreeMap / OpenMapTiles
  • Purchases: Google Play Billing
