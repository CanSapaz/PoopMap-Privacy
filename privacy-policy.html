PoopMap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

The short version

PoopMap collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. The app has no servers of its own.

Location

Your location is used on your device to show public toilets near you and to compute distances. To fetch toilets, the app sends the map area it is looking at (coordinates and a search radius) directly to OpenStreetMap's public Overpass API; map tiles come from OpenFreeMap. Like any web request, those servers see your IP address and the requested area. PoopMap sends no identifiers, no account and no device data with these requests, and we never receive your location.

Data stored on your device

Saved toilets, personal notes, offline area packs and settings are stored locally on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

PoopMap Pro (one-time purchase) and tips are processed entirely by the platform's own billing system: Google Play Billing on Android and Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit) on iOS. We operate no purchase servers and receive no personal payment information. Your Pro entitlement is tied to your store account and restored by the store ("Restore purchase" in the app).

Permissions

  • Location (while using the app) — to find toilets near you.
  • Internet — to load the map and toilet data.

That is the complete list.

Third-party services

  • Toilet data: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), via the Overpass API
  • Map tiles: OpenFreeMap / OpenMapTiles
  • Purchases: Google Play Billing (Android), Apple In-App Purchase (iOS)

Children

PoopMap is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
